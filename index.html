    Right now, horror is seriously trending. It’s wild how many low‑budget horror films are becoming massive hits — selling out, breaking records, and sometimes earning up to 100 times what they cost. So yeah, this genre is basically on fire. But we’re not here for the basic horror movies that just spam jump scares. Instead, we’ve got films with either a fresh, unexpected story or visuals and camera work that truly stand out. They keep you tense the smart way with story and style, not with the same old spooky tropes.
